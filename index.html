// 01 Overview

Bid smart. Build clever.

A team-based electronics and innovation event where participants bid for components using virtual credits and then build their own creative working project using the resources they acquire.

Objective

To encourage participants to think creatively, make strategic engineering decisions and develop a functional project under limited resources, budget and time constraints.

Top 3 teams get to keep what they build.

// 02 Format

Spend wisely. Every credit counts.

STEP 01

Each team receives a fixed amount of virtual credits.

STEP 02

Common/basic components are bought using these credits.

STEP 03

Special/limited components are auctioned and can be bought using the credits left after buying the basic components.

STEP 04

Teams must strategically bid and manage their budget.

STEP 05

After the auction, teams can build any project they want using only the components/resources they acquired.

STEP 06

Teams design, assemble, program and demonstrate their project.

STEP 07

Projects are evaluated on functionality, creativity, technical implementation and efficient use of resources.

Who can join

Who can join

Team rules

// 04 Rules

Play fair, build smart.

01Each team will receive the same initial amount of virtual credits.
02Teams must use only the components/resources they have acquired through the permitted event mechanisms.
03Teams may not secretly obtain components from other teams or external sources.
04Any resource trade must be approved by the event coordinators.
05Components cannot be exchanged after the trading period closes.
06Teams must not interfere with another team's circuit, code, components, or workspace.
07Any attempt to gain an unfair advantage through unauthorized hardware, software, communication, or external assistance may result in disqualification.
08Only low-voltage components supplied/approved by the organizers may be used.
09Teams are responsible for handling the components assigned to them.
10The organizers' decision regarding rule violations and scoring will be final.
